# Apple trades at elevated valuation multiples despite robust profitability metrics

Apple Inc. is valued at USD 5.03 trillion with a current price of 344.63, supported by strong underlying profitability: gross margin of 48.65%, operating margin of 32.62%, and profit margin of 27.62%. The exceptionally high return on equity of 148.75% reflects efficient capital deployment, yet investors are pricing in significant future growth expectations relative to current earnings power.

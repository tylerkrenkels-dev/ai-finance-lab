# Treasuries Rally on Softer Momentum; Gold Surges While Oil Retreats

US Treasury yields declined sharply, with the 2-year falling 5.0 basis points and the 10-year dropping 7.0 basis points, as the 2s10s curve steepened to 43.0 basis points. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.63%. Gold rallied 6.06% in a single day to 4343.5 USD/oz, while crude oil sold off—Brent falling 8.3% to 88.9 USD/bbl and WTI declining 4.87% to 81.96 USD/bbl. Copper extended gains with a 10.11% monthly advance to 6.7325 USD/lb. The ASX 200 climbed 0.88% to 9284.5, supported by broader risk appetite. Several key data series remain stale, including US CPI, Australian bond yields, and commodity benchmarks.

- US 2-year Treasury yield fell to 4.2%, down 5.0 basis points on the day and 6.0 basis points over the week, though up 6.0 basis points on the month.
- US 10-year Treasury yield declined to 4.63%, down 7.0 basis points daily and 2.0 basis points weekly, but up 14.0 basis points monthly.
- The US 2s10s curve slope widened to 43.0 basis points as longer-dated yields underperformed shorter-dated ones.
- Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.63%, unchanged across all measured periods.
- Gold surged 6.06% to 4343.5 USD/oz in a single session, with weekly and monthly gains of 7.65% and 5.61% respectively.
- Brent Crude Oil fell sharply to 88.9 USD/bbl, down 8.3% daily and 3.18% weekly, though up 29.44% over the month.
- WTI Crude Oil declined 4.87% to 81.96 USD/bbl on the day, with a 17.54% monthly gain.
- Copper advanced to 6.7325 USD/lb, up 10.11% on the month and 7.32% over the week.
- ASX 200 gained 0.88% to 9284.5, with a 5.14% monthly advance. Note: Australian bond yields and AUD/USD are stale as of 2026-07-29 and 2026-07-31 respectively; US CPI, iron ore, and coal data have not been updated since 2026-06-01.

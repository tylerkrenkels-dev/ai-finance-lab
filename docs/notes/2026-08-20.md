# Gold Surges While Long-End Rates Retreat; Commodity Strength Tempered by Stale Data

Gold rallied sharply to 4574.2998 USD/oz with a 1.89% daily gain, extending a 14.06% monthly advance amid broader risk-off sentiment. US 10-Year Treasury yields fell 6.0 basis points to 4.65%, while the 2s10s curve remains modest at 46.0 basis points. The US 2-Year held steady at 4.19%, and the Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.63%. Brent crude climbed 3.09% to 95.29 USD/bbl on the day, though WTI advanced more modestly at 0.51%. The ASX 200 declined 0.18% to 9053.7998, while copper edged down 0.05% to 6.484 USD/lb. Several key datasets remain stale, including Australian bond yields (as of 2026-08-12), AUD/USD (as of 2026-08-14), US CPI (as of 2026-07-01), and commodity prices for iron ore and coal (as of 2026-07-01).

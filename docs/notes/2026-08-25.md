# Gold Surges While Rates Stabilize; Australian Assets Gain on Currency Strength

AUD/USD strengthened to 0.7178, delivering annualized carry returns of 298.05% over one day and 29.28% over one month, supported by a 0.72% rate differential. The ASX 200 climbed 0.49% to 9103.0996, while copper and oil prices also advanced.

# Oil Surges on Risk Appetite While Rates Hold Steady; Commodity Strength Broadens

Energy markets led a broad commodity rally, with Brent crude jumping 6.99% and WTI rising 5.11% as risk sentiment improved. Gold climbed 1.97% and copper gained 1.54%, signaling renewed appetite for growth-linked assets. US Treasury yields remained anchored, with the 2-year at 4.39% and 10-year at 4.79%, while the Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.63%. The US 2s10s curve maintained a 40.0 basis-point slope. Australian rates data is stale, with the 3-year yield last recorded at 4.6% and the 10-year at 5.02% as of 2026-08-26. The ASX 200 edged lower by 0.1%, while AUD/USD weakness persisted at 0.7166, though month-to-date carry returns remain positive at 31.4%.

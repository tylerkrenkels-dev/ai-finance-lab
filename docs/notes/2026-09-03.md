# Commodity Rally Drives Risk-On Sentiment as Rates Hold Steady

Energy and precious metals surged overnight, with gold posting a 3.59% gain and crude oils advancing sharply, while US Treasury yields remained flat at 4.39% for the 2-year and 4.79% for the 10-year. The 2s10s curve maintained a 40.0 basis-point slope. Australian equities retreated 0.97% on the day, though the ASX 200 remains marginally positive on a one-month basis. AUD/USD carry dynamics have shifted from negative to positive on a monthly horizon, with the annualised return reaching 31.4% over the past month despite a 0.72% rate differential favoring the US dollar.

- Gold rallied 3.59% to 4523.2002 USD/oz, extending monthly gains to 12.14%, while copper advanced 2.68% to 6.675 USD/lb.
- Brent crude jumped 6.99% to 96.02 USD/bbl and WTI gained 5.11% to 91.48 USD/bbl, both posting strong weekly and monthly advances.
- US 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields held flat at 4.39% and 4.79% respectively, with the federal funds rate steady at 3.63%.
- The US 2s10s curve slope remained at 40.0 basis points, while the AU 3s10s slope stood at 42.5 basis points as of 2026-08-26.
- ASX 200 declined 0.97% to 8978.4004 on the day but remains up 0.02% over the month.
- AUD/USD carry shifted to positive territory on a one-month basis with an annualised return of 31.4%, though the one-day window showed a -161.55% annualised return.
- Henry Hub natural gas rose 7.41% over the week to 2.9 USD/MMBtu, with one-month gains of 11.97%.
- Australian bond yields are stale as of 2026-08-26; AU 3-year yields stood at 4.6% and AU 10-year yields at 5.02%, with the AU-US 10Y spread at 23.3 basis points.
- US CPI data is stale as of 2026-07-01; iron ore and coal prices have not been updated since that date.

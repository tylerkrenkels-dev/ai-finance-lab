# Treasuries Consolidate as Australian Yields Surge; Commodity Strength Persists Despite Data Gaps

US Treasury yields edged lower on the day, with the 2-year falling 5.0 basis points to 4.34% and the 10-year declining 2.0 basis points to 4.77%, though both remain elevated on a weekly and monthly basis. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.63%. Australian yields posted sharper gains, with the 3-year climbing 10.1 basis points to 4.82% and the 10-year rising 5.1 basis points to 5.22%, while the RBA Cash Rate remained anchored at 4.35%. The US 2s10s curve slope stands at 43.0 basis points, while the AU 3s10s slope is 40.1 basis points. Gold rallied 1.06% to 4476.6001 USD/oz, and copper advanced 1.3% to 6.6825 USD/lb. The ASX 200 declined 0.16% to 9005.9004. Several commodity and FX data points are stale, with US CPI last updated 67 days ago, and crude oil benchmarks last refreshed 5 days ago.

- US 2-year Treasury yield at 4.34%, down 5.0 basis points on the day but up 14.0 basis points over the week and 9.0 basis points over the month.
- US 10-year Treasury yield at 4.77%, down 2.0 basis points on the day but up 10.0 basis points over the week and 7.0 basis points over the month.
- Federal Funds Rate unchanged at 3.63% across all measured periods.
- Australian 3-year government bond yield at 4.82%, up 10.1 basis points on the day and 22.1 basis points over the week, with a 32.8 basis point monthly gain.
- Australian 10-year government bond yield at 5.22%, up 5.1 basis points on the day and 19.7 basis points over the week, with a 27.7 basis point monthly gain.
- AU-US 10-year spread at 45.0 basis points, reflecting a widening premium for Australian duration.
- Gold at 4476.6001 USD/oz, up 1.06% on the day and 5.53% over the month despite a 0.78% weekly decline.
- Copper at 6.6825 USD/lb, up 1.3% on the day and 0.66% over the week, essentially flat on the month.
- ASX 200 at 9005.9004, down 0.16% on the day, 0.95% over the week, and 1.53% over the month.

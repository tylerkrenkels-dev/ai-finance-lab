# Treasuries Consolidate as Curve Steepens; Commodities Rally Moderates with Gold and Copper Steady

US Treasury yields retreated modestly on the day, with the 2-year falling 5.0 basis points to 4.34% and the 10-year declining 2.0 basis points to 4.77%, steepening the 2s10s curve to 43.0 basis points. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.63%. Australian yields have risen more substantially over the month, with the 3-year at 4.82% and 10-year at 5.22%, though data through 2 September is now stale. The AU-US 10-year spread stands at 45.0 basis points. Commodity markets showed mixed momentum: gold at 4453.6001 USD/oz retreated 0.51% on the day but remains up 2.6% over the month, while copper at 6.721 USD/lb gained 0.58% daily and 2.29% monthly. Oil prices have stalled after sharp weekly gains, with Brent at 96.02 USD/bbl and WTI at 91.48 USD/bbl, though both readings are stale as of 1 September. The ASX 200 declined 0.16% to 9005.9004, extending monthly losses. AUD/USD carry has turned positive on a one-month horizon at 31.4% annualised, though near-term volatility remains elevated.

- US 2-year Treasury yield fell 5.0 basis points to 4.34%, while the 10-year declined 2.0 basis points to 4.77%, with the 2s10s curve steepening to 43.0 basis points.
- Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.63% with no movement over the past month.
- Australian 3-year and 10-year yields stand at 4.82% and 5.22% respectively, having risen 32.8 basis points and 27.7 basis points over the month, though data is stale as of 2 September.
- AU 3s10s curve slope at 40.1 basis points; AU-US 10-year spread at 45.0 basis points.
- Gold at 4453.6001 USD/oz declined 0.51% on the day but gained 2.6% over one month.

- Brent Crude at 96.02 USD/bbl and WTI at 91.48 USD/bbl have stalled after sharp weekly rallies, with data stale as of 1 September.
- AUD/USD carry turned positive at 31.4% annualised over one month, though one-week carry remains negative at -8.0% annualised.
- ASX 200 declined 0.16% to 9005.9004, with monthly losses of 1.53%. US CPI, Australian commodity prices, and several energy benchmarks carry significant data staleness.

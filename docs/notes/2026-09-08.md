# US Rates Rise Modestly While Australian Yields Steepen; Gold Retreats as Copper Advances

US Treasury yields moved higher this week, with the 2-year climbing 3.0 basis points to 4.37% and the 10-year rising 1.0 basis point to 4.78%, keeping the 2s10s curve at a modest 41.0 basis point slope. The Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.63%. Australian yields have steepened significantly over the month, with the 3-year at 4.82% and 10-year at 5.22%, though data is stale as of 2026-09-02. The AU-US 10-year spread stands at 44.0 basis points. In commodities, gold declined 1.26% to 4397.2998 USD/oz, while copper gained 0.81% to 6.7755 USD/lb. Oil prices remain elevated but data is dated; Brent was last at 96.02 USD/bbl and WTI at 91.48 USD/bbl as of 2026-09-01. The ASX 200 edged up 0.06% to 9010.9004, though it remains down 2.73% over the month. AUD/USD carry trades show annualised returns of 51.42% over one day, 32.01% over one week, and 29.33% over one month, driven by a 0.72% rate differential.

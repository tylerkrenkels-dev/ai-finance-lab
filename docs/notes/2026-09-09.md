# Treasuries Rise on Broad Momentum; Commodities Surge While Equities Retreat

US Treasury yields advanced across the curve, with the 2-year climbing 2.0 basis points and the 10-year rising 2.0 basis points, maintaining a 41.0 basis-point slope. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.63%, while the RBA Cash Rate remained anchored at 4.35%. Precious metals and industrial metals rallied sharply, with gold reaching 4445.0 USD/oz and copper climbing to 6.8575 USD/lb. The ASX 200 declined 1.0% on the day, extending losses over the month. AUD/USD carry trades showed elevated annualized returns across all windows, though FX data is five days stale. Several commodity and inflation readings remain significantly outdated.

- Gold surged 1.16% to 4445.0 USD/oz on the day and 2.4% over the month, while copper advanced 1.76% daily and 4.37% monthly to 6.8575 USD/lb.
- Brent Crude Oil at 96.02 USD/bbl is up 6.99% from 2026-08-28 and up 8.82% over the week, though data is stale as of 2026-09-01; WTI Crude at 91.48 USD/bbl has gained 5.11% daily and 6.17% monthly.
- AUD/USD carry trades annualized at 51.42% over one day, 32.01% over one week, and 29.33% over one month, with a consistent rate differential of 0.72%, though spot data is five days old.
- The ASX 200 fell 1.0% to 8920.7998 on the day and 3.7% over the month, signaling weakness in Australian equities.
- AU 3-year and 10-year government bond yields at 4.82% and 5.22% respectively are stale as of 2026-09-02; US CPI data is stale as of 2026-07-01 (70 days ago).
- Henry Hub Natural Gas at 2.9 USD/MMBtu is flat daily but up 11.97% over the month; Iron Ore and Coal (Australia) data are significantly outdated as of 2026-07-01.
- The Federal Funds Rate and RBA Cash Rate have remained unchanged over all measured periods at 3.63% and 4.35% respectively.

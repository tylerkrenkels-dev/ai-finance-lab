# Oil Surges While Metals Retreat; US Rates Edge Higher as Curve Remains Flat

Energy markets dominated overnight moves, with Brent crude climbing to 109.51 USD/bbl and WTI reaching 97.26 USD/bbl on strong monthly gains. Precious metals and industrial commodities reversed course, with gold declining and copper falling sharply. US Treasury yields edged higher across the curve, with the 2-year at 4.43% and 10-year at 4.83%, while the 2s10s slope held steady at 40.0 basis points. The Federal Funds rate remained anchored at 3.63%. Australian bond yields and the RBA cash rate target were last updated on 2 September and 9 September respectively, with the AU 3s10s slope at 40.1 basis points. AUD/USD carry trades showed annualised returns of 51.42% over one day, 32.01% over one week, and 29.33% over one month, though FX data is current only through 4 September.

- Brent crude surged 3.19% to 109.51 USD/bbl on the day and 24.98% over the month, while WTI gained 3.24% daily and 21.93% monthly to 97.26 USD/bbl.
- Gold retreated 1.29% to 4359.1001 USD/oz on the day and 2.95% over the week, while copper fell 4.2% to 6.5175 USD/lb.
- US 2-year Treasury yield rose 4.0 basis points to 4.43%, and the 10-year climbed 3.0 basis points to 4.83%, with the 2s10s slope at 40.0 basis points.
- The US Effective Federal Funds Rate remained unchanged at 3.63% across all measured periods.
- RBA Cash Rate Target held steady at 4.35%, while AU 3-year and 10-year yields stood at 4.82% and 5.22% respectively as of 2 September.
- AU-US 10-year spread measured 39.0 basis points, with AU 3s10s slope at 40.1 basis points.
- AUD/USD carry annualised returns reached 51.42% over one day, driven by a 0.72% rate differential and 50.7% annualised spot appreciation.
- ASX 200 declined 0.11% to 8911.4004 on the day and 3.8% over the month.

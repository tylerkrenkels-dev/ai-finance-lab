# Rates Rise Across Curves as Oil Surges; Australian Equities Retreat

US Treasury yields climbed across the curve, with the 2-year rising 13.0 basis points and the 10-year advancing 12.0 basis points, keeping the 2s10s slope at 39.0 basis points. The RBA Cash Rate Target remained anchored at 4.35%, while Australian government bond yields edged higher, with the 3-year at 4.82% and the 10-year at 5.2%. Brent crude surged 3.19% to 109.51 USD/bbl, while WTI climbed 3.24% to 97.26 USD/bbl. Gold and copper posted modest gains, though the ASX 200 declined 0.89% to 8741.2002. AUD/USD carry trades remain attractive at 29.33% annualised over one month, supported by a 0.72% rate differential. Several commodity and FX data points are stale, with US CPI last updated 43 days ago, AUD/USD 9 days ago, and Australian coal and iron ore 74 days ago.

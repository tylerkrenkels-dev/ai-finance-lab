# US Rates Rise Across the Curve as Equities Retreat; Commodity Strength Persists Despite Data Gaps

US Treasury yields advanced across the curve over the past month, with the 2-year climbing 48.0 basis points to 4.65% and the 10-year rising 29.0 basis points to 4.97%, while the Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.63%. The US 2s10s curve slope stands at 32.0 basis points. Australian rates data through September 9 shows the 3-year yield at 4.82% and the 10-year at 5.2%, with the AU 3s10s slope at 38.2 basis points; the AU-US 10Y spread is 23.2 basis points. The RBA Cash Rate Target held steady at 4.35%. Commodity markets show mixed signals: Brent Crude Oil reached 109.51 USD/bbl as of September 9, while Gold declined to 4334.6001 USD/oz and Copper recovered to 6.468 USD/lb. The ASX 200 retreated 4.01% over the month to 8749.9004, though AUD/USD carry has turned positive on a one-month basis at 19.39% annualized. Several key data series are stale, including Australian bond yields, crude oil benchmarks, and commodity prices.

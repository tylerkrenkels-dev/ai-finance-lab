# Oil Surges While Rates Rise and Equities Retreat; Data Gaps Persist in Commodities and FX

Energy markets dominated overnight moves, with Brent crude climbing 7.88% to 130.8 USD/bbl and WTI rising 4.49% to 107.02 USD/bbl on the day.

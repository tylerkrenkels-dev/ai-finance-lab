# US Rates Rise Sharply While Equities Retreat; Commodity Strength Persists Despite Data Gaps

US Treasury yields climbed notably over the past month, with the 2-year yield reaching 4.76% and the 10-year at 5.01%, while the Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88%. AUD/USD weakened to 0.7111, with carry returns turning negative on shorter horizons. Significant data staleness affects commodity and inflation readings.

# US Rates Rise Sharply While Commodities Pull Back; Australian Data Lags

US Treasury yields climbed notably overnight, with the 2-year rising 14.0 basis points to 4.85% and the 10-year advancing 15.0 basis points to 5.11%, keeping the 2s10s curve at a modest 26.0 basis point slope. Commodity markets retreated, with Brent crude falling 1.08% to 114.89 USD/bbl and gold declining 0.21% to 4309.3999 USD/oz, though copper gained 1.45% to 6.7745 USD/lb. The ASX 200 edged higher by 0.38% to 8765.2998. Australian bond data remains stale as of 2026-09-16, and several commodity series lag significantly.

# Oil Selloff Weighs on Risk Assets as RBA Tightens; US Curve Flattens

Energy markets experienced sharp declines, with Brent crude falling 5.01% and WTI dropping 3.23% on the day, pressuring commodity-linked equities. The ASX 200 managed modest gains of 0.34% despite broader headwinds. In rates, the US 2-Year Treasury Yield declined 3.0 basis points to 4.89%, while the 10-Year rose 2.0 basis points to 5.26%, compressing the 2s10s slope to 37.0 basis points. The RBA raised its cash rate target by 25.0 basis points to 4.6%, marking the third consecutive monthly increase. Gold retreated further, down 7.2% over the month, while copper showed resilience with a 1.35% daily gain. AUD/USD carry dynamics remain volatile, with the one-day annualised return at 89.16% offset by negative one-week and one-month returns of -56.48% and -20.16% respectively.

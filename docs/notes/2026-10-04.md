# Rates Ease While Curve Steepens; Gold Retreats as Copper Stabilizes

US Treasury yields declined modestly over the past day, with the 2-year falling 10.0 basis points to 4.78% and the 10-year dropping 5.0 basis points to 5.24%, steepening the 2s10s curve to 46.0 basis points. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88%, while the RBA Cash Rate remained at 4.6% following a 25.0 basis point increase last week. Australian yields also softened, with the 3-year at 4.92% and 10-year at 5.34%, maintaining a 42.2 basis point curve slope. Gold has weakened significantly over the month, declining 6.78%, though it recovered 0.26% in the latest session. Copper showed resilience with a 1.37% daily gain. Energy markets remain under pressure, with Brent and WTI crude both down sharply on the day. The ASX 200 gained 0.79% to 8682.0996, though it remains down 3.3% over the month.

- US 2-year Treasury yield fell 10.0 basis points to 4.78% over one day, while the 10-year declined 5.0 basis points to 5.24%, with the 2s10s slope widening to 46.0 basis points.
- Federal Funds Rate remained unchanged at 3.88%, while the RBA Cash Rate held at 4.6% following a 25.0 basis point hike last week.
- Australian 3-year and 10-year yields softened to 4.92% and 5.34% respectively, with the AU-US 10-year spread at 10.4 basis points.
- Gold declined 6.78% over the month to 4173.2998 USD/oz, though it recovered 0.26% in the latest session.
- Copper gained 1.37% to 6.581 USD/lb on the day, showing relative stability despite a 2.26% weekly decline.
- Brent Crude fell 5.01% to 113.96 USD/bbl and WTI declined 3.23% to 96.16 USD/bbl, though both remain elevated on a monthly basis.
- Henry Hub Natural Gas rose 1.6% to 3.18 USD/MMBtu, up 12.77% over the month.
- AUD/USD carry returns were positive at 89.16% annualized over one day but turned negative at -56.48% annualized over one week and -20.16% annualized over one month.
- ASX 200 advanced 0.79% to 8682.0996, though it remains down 3.3% from one month ago. Note: US CPI data is stale as of 2026-08-01; AUD/USD, crude oil, and natural gas data are stale as of 2026-09-29; Iron Ore and Coal data are stale as of 2026-07-01.

# Treasuries Retreat on Softer Near-Term Outlook; Commodity Strength Persists Despite Data Gaps

US Treasury yields declined across the curve on October 8, with the 2-year falling 2.0 basis points to 4.75% and the 10-year dropping 6.0 basis points to 5.22%, though both remain elevated from a month ago. The US 2s10s curve slope stands at 47.0 basis points. Australian rates held steady, with the RBA cash rate target unchanged at 4.6% and the AU 3s10s slope at 47.1 basis points, while the AU-US 10Y spread widened to 18.4 basis points. Gold rallied to 4223.2998 USD/oz and copper advanced to 6.714 USD/lb, though several commodity and FX data points remain stale. The ASX 200 gained 0.64% to 8716.5996 on October 9, though it remains down over the month.

# US Rates Ease While Australian Yields Stabilize; Gold Retreats Amid Mixed Commodity Signals

US Treasury yields declined modestly over the past week, with the 2-year falling 6.0 basis points to 4.74% and the 10-year dropping 15.0 basis points to 5.09%, keeping the 2s10s curve at a modest 35.0 basis point slope. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88%, having risen 25.0 basis points over the month. Australian rates data through October 14 shows the RBA Cash Rate Target at 4.6% following a 25.0 basis point increase last month, while the AU 3-year and 10-year yields stand at 4.91% and 5.31% respectively, with the AU 3s10s slope at 40.5 basis points. The AU-US 10Y spread widened to 22.4 basis points. Gold has declined sharply, falling 6.12% over the month to 4154.2998 USD/oz, while copper remains relatively stable at 6.571 USD/lb. Energy markets show divergent signals, with WTI outpacing Brent over the week. The ASX 200 gained 1.0% on the day to 8806.7002, extending monthly gains. AUD/USD carry returns have turned negative on weekly and monthly horizons despite a positive rate differential of 0.72%, reflecting spot depreciation pressure.

- US 2-year Treasury yield declined 6.0 basis points over the week to 4.74%, while the 10-year fell 15.0 basis points to 5.09%, maintaining a flattened 2s10s slope of 35.0 basis points.
- The Federal Funds Rate remains at 3.88%, having risen 25.0 basis points over the past month as policy tightening continues.
- Australian 3-year and 10-year government bond yields stand at 4.91% and 5.31% respectively as of October 14, with the RBA Cash Rate Target at 4.6% following a 25.0 basis point hike last month.
- The AU-US 10Y spread widened to 22.4 basis points, reflecting relative yield positioning between the two economies.
- Gold has declined 6.12% over the month to 4154.2998 USD/oz, with a 0.99% pullback on the day, signaling reduced safe-haven demand.
- Copper held flat on the day at 6.571 USD/lb but has declined 1.9% over the week, suggesting modest demand softness.
- WTI Crude Oil gained 7.25% over the week to 101.91 USD/bbl as of October 14, outpacing Brent's more modest 0.44% weekly advance to 126.55 USD/bbl.
- AUD/USD carry returns turned negative on weekly and monthly horizons at -20.95% and -30.33% annualised respectively, despite a positive rate differential of 0.72%, driven by spot depreciation of -0.42% weekly and -2.55% monthly.
- The ASX 200 advanced 1.0% on the day to 8806.7002, with monthly gains of 1.27%, supported by broader equity market resilience. Note: US CPI data is stale as of September 1; Australian bond yields, crude oil prices, and natural gas data are stale as of October 14; Iron Ore and Coal data are stale as of July 1.

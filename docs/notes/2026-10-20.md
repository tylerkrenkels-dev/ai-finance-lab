# US Rates Rise on Steeper Curve as Commodities Mixed; Australian Data Lags

US Treasury yields climbed overnight, with the 10-year advancing 6.0 basis points to 5.15% while the 2-year rose 3.0 basis points to 4.77%, keeping the 2s10s curve steep at 38.0 basis points. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88%. Gold retreated 0.52% to 4138.2998 USD/oz, while copper gained 1.19% to 6.616 USD/lb. The ASX 200 edged up 0.44% to 8845.7002. Australian bond yields and commodity prices carry stale readings as of mid-October, and US CPI data has not been updated since September 1st.

- US 10-year Treasury yield at 5.15%, up 6.0 basis points from October 16; 2-year at 4.77%, up 3.0 basis points over the same period, with the 2s10s slope at 38.0 basis points.
- Over one month, the 10-year has risen 14.0 basis points while the 2-year gained only 1.0 basis point, reflecting a steepening bias in longer-dated yields.
- Federal Funds Rate unchanged at 3.88% across all measured windows; RBA Cash Rate Target held at 4.6%, though it rose 25.0 basis points over the past month.
- AU-US 10-year spread stands at 16.4 basis points, with Australian 3-year and 10-year yields at 4.91% and 5.31% respectively as of October 14.
- Gold declined 0.52% to 4138.2998 USD/oz on the day and 6.22% over one month; copper rose 1.19% to 6.616 USD/lb but remains down 1.44% monthly.
- AUD/USD at 0.695 USD per AUD, down 2.55% over one month; one-day carry annualised at 190.77% but one-month carry at negative 30.33%.
- ASX 200 gained 0.44% to 8845.7002 on the day and 1.31% over one month, showing modest upside momentum.
- Australian bond yields, Brent and WTI crude, natural gas, and Australian commodity prices are stale as of October 14 or earlier; US CPI data has not been updated since September 1st.

# US Rates Rise on Steeper Curve as RBA Holds; Commodity Weakness Persists

US Treasury yields moved higher across the curve, with the 10-year climbing 6.0 basis points to 5.15% while the 2-year rose 3.0 basis points to 4.77%, keeping the 2s10s slope at a modest 38.0 basis points. The RBA maintained its cash rate target at 4.6%, unchanged from the prior month despite a 25.0 basis point increase recorded one month ago. Australian government bond yields, last updated on 2026-10-14, stood at 4.91% for the 3-year and 5.31% for the 10-year, with the AU 3s10s slope at 40.5 basis points. The AU-US 10Y spread widened to 16.4 basis points. In currency markets, AUD/USD traded at 0.695, though carry returns have turned negative on weekly and monthly horizons despite a 0.72% rate differential. Commodity markets showed mixed signals: gold recovered to 4144.1001 USD/oz, copper declined to 6.55 USD/lb, and energy prices remain elevated but stale. The ASX 200 edged down 0.33% to 8816.9004 on the day.

- US 10-year Treasury yield reached 5.15%, up 6.0 basis points, while the 2-year rose 3.0 basis points to 4.77%, with the 2s10s slope holding at 38.0 basis points.
- RBA cash rate target remains at 4.6%, unchanged from the prior day and week, though up 25.0 basis points over the month.
- Australian 3-year and 10-year government bond yields stood at 4.91% and 5.31% respectively as of 2026-10-14; AU 3s10s slope at 40.5 basis points.
- AU-US 10Y spread widened to 16.4 basis points, reflecting divergent yield dynamics between the two markets.
- AUD/USD carry returns turned negative on a 1-week basis at -20.95% annualised and -30.33% on a 1-month basis, despite a 0.72% rate differential favouring the Australian dollar.
- Gold rose to 4144.1001 USD/oz, up 0.04% on the day and 0.88% over the week, though down 5.47% from one month prior.
- Copper declined to 6.55 USD/lb, falling 0.25% on the day and 2.04% over the month.
- US CPI (Year-over-Year) index stands at 336.298 as of 2026-09-01; this data is stale and last updated 50 days ago.
- ASX 200 declined 0.33% to 8816.9004 on 2026-10-20, though up 0.98% over the month. Note: Australian bond yields, AUD/USD, Brent Crude, WTI Crude, Henry Hub Natural Gas, Iron Ore, and Coal data are stale as of their respective dates.

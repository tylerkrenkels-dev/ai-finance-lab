# US Curve Flattens as Short Rates Decline; Gold Rebounds While Copper Surges

US Treasury yields moved lower across the curve, with the 2-year falling 1.0 basis point to 4.68% while the 10-year held steady at 5.0%, compressing the 2s10s slope to 32.0 basis points. The Federal Reserve maintained the effective funds rate at 3.88%, unchanged over the past month. In Australia, the RBA cash rate target stands at 4.6% following a 25.0 basis point increase over the month. Gold rallied 0.81% to 4071.3 USD/oz, reversing recent weakness, while copper gained 2.34% to 6.66 USD/lb. The AUD/USD pair traded at 0.7037, with annualized carry returns of 89.11% over one day and 65.99% over one week, though the monthly carry return moderated to 0.89%. Several commodity and Australian bond data points remain stale as of late September and early October.

# Treasuries Extend Decline While Commodities Show Mixed Signals; Australian Data Lags

US Treasury yields retreated over the past month, with the 10-year falling 18 basis points to 4.99% and the 2-year declining 11 basis points to 4.7%, though the 2-year edged up 2.0 basis points on the day. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88%, while the RBA Cash Rate remained at 4.6% after a 25 basis point increase last month. The US 2s10s curve slope stands at 29.0 basis points. Energy markets diverged sharply, with Brent crude retreating 3.92% to 123.1 USD/bbl on the day despite weekly gains, while WTI fell 1.79% to 103.37 USD/bbl. Gold declined 2.36% over the month to 4069.8999 USD/oz, and copper advanced 1.83% to 6.686 USD/lb. The ASX 200 gained 0.43% to 8888.4004, posting a 2.06% monthly advance. Several key datasets remain stale, including US CPI, Australian bond yields, and commodity prices for iron ore and coal.

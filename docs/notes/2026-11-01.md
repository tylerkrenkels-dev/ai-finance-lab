# US Yields Decline Sharply While Australian Rates Hold Steady; Gold Retreats Amid Mixed Commodity Signals

US Treasury yields fell notably over the past day, with the 2-year dropping 6.0 basis points to 4.61% and the 10-year declining 7.0 basis points to 4.98%, while the Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.88%. The US 2s10s curve slope stands at 37.0 basis points. In Australia, the RBA Cash Rate held at 4.6%, though the 3-year and 10-year government bond yields both rose on the day, with the AU 3s10s slope at 29.2 basis points. The AU-US 10Y spread widened to 22.8 basis points. Gold declined significantly over the month to 3905.3999 USD/oz, while copper showed modest resilience at 6.5025 USD/lb. The ASX 200 edged higher to 8908.0996. Several commodity and FX data points are stale, and US CPI data has not been updated since September.

- US 2-year Treasury yield fell 6.0 basis points to 4.61% and the 10-year declined 7.0 basis points to 4.98%, with both yields down over the month by 28.0 basis points each.
- The US 2s10s curve slope remains modest at 37.0 basis points, while the Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88% across all measured periods.
- Australian 3-year and 10-year government bond yields rose on the day to 4.92% and 5.21% respectively, despite the RBA Cash Rate remaining unchanged at 4.6%.
- The AU 3s10s slope stands at 29.2 basis points, and the AU-US 10Y spread widened to 22.8 basis points.
- Gold retreated to 3905.3999 USD/oz, down 7.07% over the month, while copper held relatively steady at 6.5025 USD/lb with a 0.32% monthly gain.
- AUD/USD carry on a one-month basis annualised 0.89%, driven by a 0.72% rate differential and minimal spot appreciation of 0.17% annualised.
- The ASX 200 advanced to 8908.0996, up 1.35% over the month and 0.18% on the day.
- Brent Crude Oil, WTI Crude Oil, Henry Hub Natural Gas, US CPI, Iron Ore, and Coal data are stale as of 2026-10-27 or earlier; AUD/USD data is stale as of 2026-10-23.

# Treasuries Extend Decline as Fed Holds Steady; Gold and Copper Retreat

US Treasury yields fell over the past month, with the 10-year declining 37.0 basis points to 4.91% and the 2-year down 24.0 basis points to 4.59%, while the Federal Reserve maintained its effective funds rate at 3.88%. The 2s10s curve remains modestly steep at 32.0 basis points. Precious metals and industrial metals have weakened, with gold down 5.49% over the month and copper declining 1.62%. The ASX 200 has gained 1.99% over the past month despite a flat session today. Several commodity and Australian data points remain stale, with US CPI data last updated 64 days ago.

- US 10-year Treasury yield fell 37.0 basis points over the month to 4.91%, while the 2-year declined 24.0 basis points to 4.59%, steepening the 2s10s curve to 32.0 basis points.
- The Federal Reserve held the effective funds rate steady at 3.88% with no movement over the past month, while the RBA maintained its cash rate target at 4.6%.

- The AU-US 10-year spread stands at 29.8 basis points, reflecting a modest yield premium for Australian debt.
- Gold declined 5.49% over the month to 3933.7 USD/oz, while copper fell 1.62% to 6.387 USD/lb.
- Brent Crude Oil traded at 123.1 USD/bbl as of 2026-10-27 (8 days stale), up 6.11% over the month but down 3.92% in the most recent day.
- AUD/USD stands at 0.7038 as of 2026-10-30 (5 days stale), with one-month carry returning 15.77% annualised despite a one-day carry of -4.47%.
- The ASX 200 gained 1.99% over the past month to 8855.2998, though it was essentially flat today.
- US CPI year-over-year data is stale as of 2026-09-01 (64 days ago), and Australian commodity prices including iron ore and coal have not been updated since that date.

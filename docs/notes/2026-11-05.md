# Gold Surges While Oil Retreats; RBA Tightens as US Rates Edge Higher

Gold rallied sharply with a 4.1% single-day gain to 4101.7998 USD/oz, while crude oil prices declined across both benchmarks over the month. The RBA raised its cash rate target by 25.0 basis points to 4.85%, marking a significant policy shift, even as the US Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88%. US Treasury yields moved modestly higher on the day, with the 2-year at 4.61% and 10-year at 4.95%, though both remain lower over the month. The ASX 200 gained 1.17% to close at 8958.7002, supported by broad equity strength. Several key data series remain stale, including US CPI, Australian bond yields, and commodity prices for iron ore and coal.

- Brent crude oil fell 0.34% to 107.03 USD/bbl on the day and has declined 21.02% over the month; WTI crude retreated 0.42% to 99.64 USD/bbl and is up 1.79% monthly.
- The ASX 200 advanced 1.17% to 8958.7002 on 2026-11-05, with gains of 0.75% over the week and 3.13% over the month.

# Yields Decline Across Developed Markets as RBA Tightens; Commodity Weakness Persists

Gold recovered 4.28% over the week to 4072.7 USD/oz, while copper declined 3.8% on the month. The ASX 200 gained 3.26% over the past month but retreated 0.49% in the latest session.

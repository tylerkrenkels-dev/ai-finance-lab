# Australian equities surge while US rates stabilize; RBA holds as AUD weakens sharply

The ASX 200 rallied 1.13% on the day to 9131.7998, extending monthly gains to 4.76%, as risk appetite improved. US Treasury yields held relatively steady with the 2-year at 4.51% and 10-year at 4.86%, while the Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.88%. The RBA maintained its cash rate target at 4.85% following a 25 basis point hike last week. The Australian dollar weakened materially, with AUD/USD falling to 0.6875, generating negative carry returns across all measured windows. Gold rallied sharply to 4123.8999 USD/oz, while copper recovered 1.56% on the day. Several commodity and inflation data points remain stale, limiting the completeness of the macro picture.

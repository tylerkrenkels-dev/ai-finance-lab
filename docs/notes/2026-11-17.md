# US Rates Rise Sharply While Australian Assets Weaken; Commodity Losses Accelerate

US Treasury yields climbed notably over the past week, with the 2-year rising 37.0 basis points and the 10-year advancing 29.0 basis points, while the Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.88%. The RBA Cash Rate held steady at 4.85% following a 25.0 basis-point increase last month. Australian government bond yields, last updated on 2026-11-11, stood at 4.73% for the 3-year and 5.04% for the 10-year. The AUD/USD exchange rate declined to 0.6775, with carry returns deteriorating sharply across all horizons. Precious metals and industrial metals extended losses, while the ASX 200 fell 1.51% on the day. Several commodity and Australian data points remain stale.

- US 2-year Treasury yield reached 4.88%, up 37.0 basis points over one week and 2.0 basis points on the day; 10-year yield climbed to 5.15%, gaining 29.0 basis points weekly and 5.0 basis points daily.

- AUD/USD weakened to 0.6775, down 0.4 basis points on the day and 1.0 basis point over one week; one-month carry return deteriorated to -34.26% annualized.
- Gold fell to 3865.1001 USD/oz, declining 1.0% on the day and 7.56% over one month; copper dropped to 5.9865 USD/lb, down 1.63% daily and 8.34% monthly.
- Brent Crude Oil stood at 92.14 USD/bbl as of 2026-11-11, up 2.2% on the day but down 29.14% over one month; WTI Crude at 89.62 USD/bbl showed a 9.23% monthly decline.
- Henry Hub Natural Gas held at 3.0 USD/MMBtu as of 2026-11-11, with a 14.07% weekly gain offset by a 3.23% monthly loss.

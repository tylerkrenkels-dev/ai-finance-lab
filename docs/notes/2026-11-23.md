# Treasuries Rally on Softer Momentum; Gold Rebounds While Copper Stabilizes

US Treasury yields declined across the curve, with the 2-year falling 4.0 basis points and the 10-year dropping 3.0 basis points, keeping the 2s10s slope at 28.0 basis points. The Federal Funds Rate held steady at 3.88%. Australian rates also retreated, though data through mid-November shows the RBA Cash Rate Target unchanged at 4.85% following a 25.0 basis point increase over the prior month. Gold surged 1.72% in the latest session after a sharp monthly decline, while copper recovered 1.07% daily despite weakness over longer horizons. The ASX 200 edged up 0.08% but remains under pressure on a weekly basis. AUD/USD carry dynamics remain challenged, with annualised returns of -186.62% over one day and -34.26% over one month as the currency weakens against a higher-yielding dollar. Several commodity and FX datasets are stale, and US CPI data has not been updated since October 1.

# US Yields Retreat While Australian Equities Rally; Commodity Volatility Persists

US Treasury yields declined over the past week, with the 2-year falling 17.0 basis points and the 10-year dropping 19.0 basis points, while the Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.88%. The US 2s10s curve maintained a modest 28.0 basis point slope. In Australia, the RBA Cash Rate held steady at 4.85% following a 25.0 basis point increase over the month, though Australian bond data through 2026-11-18 shows the 3s10s slope at 34.3 basis points. The ASX 200 gained 0.94% on the day to 8773.9004, supported by strength in commodities: copper surged 3.43% to 5.5465 USD/lb and gold rallied 1.88% to 3628.7 USD/oz, though both remain down significantly over the month. Oil prices recovered modestly, with WTI up 1.01% to 82.24 USD/bbl and Brent up 0.66% to 91.8 USD/bbl, though both face substantial monthly declines. The AUD/USD carry trade remains deeply negative across all horizons, with annualised returns of -179.56% over one day and -60.39% over one month, reflecting both the 0.97% rate differential and sharp spot depreciation.

# US Yields Retreat While Commodities Rally; Australian Rates Held Steady

US Treasury yields declined over the past week, with the 2-year falling 17.0 basis points and the 10-year dropping 19.0 basis points, while the Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.88%. The RBA held its cash rate target at 4.85%, having raised 25.0 basis points over the past month. Commodity markets showed mixed momentum: gold surged 3.28% in a single day to 3651.6001 USD/oz, copper jumped 6.07% to 5.656 USD/lb, and natural gas climbed 18.71% over the month to 3.3 USD/MMBtu, while crude oil retreated sharply with Brent down 28.45% and WTI down 25.05% over the month. The ASX 200 gained 0.67% on the day and 2.67% over the week. AUD/USD weakness persisted, with the carry trade showing negative annualised returns across all windows, driven by a 0.97% rate differential offset by sustained spot depreciation.

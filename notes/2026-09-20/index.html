    US Rates Decline While Curve Flattens; Energy Rallies Sharply as Commodity Volatility Persists

    US Treasury yields retreated on the day, with the 2-year falling 7.0 basis points to 4.67% and the 10-year declining 7.0 basis points to 4.94%, leaving the 2s10s curve at a modest 27.0 basis point slope. The Federal Reserve's effective funds rate rose 25.0 basis points to 3.88%, reflecting recent policy action. Australian yields also softened, with the 3-year at 4.99% and 10-year at 5.35%, while the RBA cash rate remained anchored at 4.35%. Energy markets surged notably, with Brent crude up 7.88% to 130.8 USD/bbl and WTI gaining 4.49% to 107.02 USD/bbl over recent sessions. Gold retreated 0.27% to 4412.8999 USD/oz, while copper advanced 1.48% to 6.713 USD/lb. The ASX 200 was essentially flat on the day at 8731.2002. Several commodity and FX data points carry staleness warnings.

    • US 2-year Treasury yield fell 7.0 basis points to 4.67%, while the 10-year declined 7.0 basis points to 4.94%, with the 2s10s slope at 27.0 basis points.
    • The US effective federal funds rate rose 25.0 basis points to 3.88% across all measured windows, signaling recent monetary tightening.
    • Australian 3-year and 10-year yields declined on the day to 4.99% and 5.35% respectively, while the RBA cash rate target remained steady at 4.35%.
    • The AU-US 10-year spread stands at 40.8 basis points, with the AU 3s10s curve at 35.8 basis points.
    • Brent crude oil surged 7.88% to 130.8 USD/bbl on the day and 42.14% over the month, while WTI gained 4.49% to 107.02 USD/bbl.
    • Copper advanced 1.48% to 6.713 USD/lb on the day and 3.92% over the month, outpacing gold which declined 0.27% to 4412.8999 USD/oz.
    • AUD/USD carry returns were mixed: 15.74% annualised over one day but negative 24.85% annualised over one week, with the rate differential at 0.47%.
    • The ASX 200 was essentially flat on the day at 8731.2002, down 0.01%, though down 3.74% over the month.
    • Multiple commodity and FX data points are stale: US CPI last updated 2026-08-01 (50 days ago), AUD/USD as of 2026-09-11 (9 days ago), crude oil and natural gas as of 2026-09-15 (5 days ago), and iron ore and coal as of 2026-07-01 (81 days ago).

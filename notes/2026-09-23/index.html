    Rates Steady as Curve Flattens; Commodities Retreat Amid Mixed Signals

    US Treasury yields held relatively stable on the day, with the 2-year at 4.71% and 10-year at 4.96%, though the 2-year declined 5.0 basis points from the prior session. The US 2s10s curve remains modest at 25.0 basis points. The Federal Funds Rate stands at 3.88%, up 25.0 basis points over the past month. Commodity markets showed divergent momentum: oil prices retreated with Brent at 114.89 USD/bbl and WTI at 96.41 USD/bbl, while gold declined to 4323.2002 USD/oz. Copper recovered modestly to 6.79 USD/lb. Australian data is partially stale, with bond yields last updated 7 days ago; the RBA Cash Rate Target remains anchored at 4.35%. AUD/USD carry has turned negative on shorter horizons, with 1-week annualised returns at -45.32%, though 1-month carry stands at 2.79%.

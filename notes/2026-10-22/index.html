    Commodity Selloff Weighs on Risk Assets as US Rates Stabilize

    Precious metals and energy prices retreated sharply overnight, with gold declining 2.6% and copper falling 2.15%, while crude oil benchmarks softened across the board. US Treasury yields held relatively steady, with the 2-year at 4.81% and 10-year at 5.13%, maintaining a 32 basis-point 2s10s slope. The Federal Funds Rate remained anchored at 3.88%, while the RBA's cash rate target held at 4.6% following a 25 basis-point increase over the past month. Australian equities posted modest gains, with the ASX 200 rising 0.16% on the day, though commodity-linked assets face headwinds from broader price weakness.

    • Gold fell 2.6% to 4042.2 USD/oz, extending a month-long decline of 7.64%, as risk-off sentiment gripped markets.
    • Copper declined 2.15% to 6.4025 USD/lb, with losses accelerating over the one-month window to 5.28%.
    • Brent crude oil dropped 3.03% to 115.22 USD/bbl, while WTI crude fell 1.93% to 107.89 USD/bbl, though WTI remains up 13.1% over the past week.
    • Henry Hub natural gas retreated 5.49% to 3.1 USD/MMBtu on the day, despite a 4.38% monthly gain.
    • US 2-year Treasury yield rose 2.0 basis points to 4.81%, while the 10-year gained 1.0 basis point to 5.13%, with the curve maintaining a 32.0 basis-point slope.
    • The RBA cash rate target remained at 4.6% following a 25.0 basis-point increase over the past month, while Australian government bond yields are stale as of 2026-10-14.
    • AUD/USD carry returns turned negative on a one-week basis at -20.95% annualized and -30.33% on a one-month basis, despite a 0.72% rate differential.
    • The ASX 200 gained 0.16% to 8831.2998, with weekly and monthly performance showing gains of 1.18% and 1.14% respectively.
    • US CPI data is stale as of 2026-09-01, Australian bond yields and AUD/USD are stale as of 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-16 respectively, and commodity prices for iron ore and coal have not been updated since 2026-09-01.
